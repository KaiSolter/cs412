About Paul Morphy

Paul Morphy was an American chess master who lived from June 22, 1837 to July 10, 1884. He is considered to be the greatest chess player of his era. He was undefeated in official matches, before he retired from chess and public life at the age of 22. Although the official Chess World Championship did not exist during his time, Morphy is often considered to be the first world chess champion. He was known for his brilliant queen sacrifices and strong attacking style.
